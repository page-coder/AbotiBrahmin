Privacy, archival ethics & transparency

This encyclopedia is a non-commercial, public-interest cultural reference archive.

No tracking

The site uses no advertising trackers, analytics, visitor counters or cookies of its own. Search runs entirely in your browser.

What is stored in your browser

Only your reading preferences, in your browser’s local storage: aboti_theme (light/dark), aboti_fontsize (text size) and aboti_lang (the last language link you chose). These never leave your device and can be cleared in your browser settings.

Third-party resources

  • Google Fonts. Pages load typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, so your browser sends a request (including your IP address) to Google. If it is blocked, pages fall back to system fonts.
  • GitHub Pages. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep its own server logs.
  • External source links open third-party websites that have their own policies.

Redaction of personal information

Public community directories have historically listed member phone numbers and residential addresses. This encyclopedia redacts private contact information of living individuals and publishes only aggregate sociological and historical data, such as surname categories and ancestral villages. Unconsented family trees are not published.

AbotiBrahmin Digital Encyclopedia

An independent, non-commercial, bilingual digital reference archive documenting the history, sacred geography, traditions, and social development of the Aboti Brahmin community in Dwarka and Gujarat.
