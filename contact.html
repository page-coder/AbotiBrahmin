Corrections, contributions & contact

The AbotiBrahmin Digital Encyclopedia welcomes verifiable historical additions, transcriptions of copper plates or stone inscriptions, and corrections supported by reliable primary or secondary sources.

What to send

  • Source requirement: the exact book title, author, publication year and page number, or an uncropped scan of the historical inscription or state record.
  • Epistemic tagging: oral family accounts and barot chopda entries are welcomed and labelled [Traditional Account] or [Family Record] until independently corroborated.
  • Privacy protection: personal phone numbers, residential addresses and unconsented details of living individuals are redacted.

Where to send it

  • Open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository: https://github.com/page-coder/AbotiBrahmin/issues
GitHub issues are public. Do not include phone numbers, addresses or other private details of living people in a public issue.
