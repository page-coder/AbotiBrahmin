Aboti Brahmin Surnames & Gotra Affiliations

Documented family names, village associations in Saurashtra, alternative spellings, and genealogical cautions.

Genealogical Methodological Note: In Gujarat, several surnames (such as Joshi, Mehta, Odedara, or Tukadiya) are shared across multiple distinct communities (including other Brahmin branches, agrarian, and merchant groups). A surname alone does not prove community membership. Historical affiliation requires corroboration via family vanshavali, kuldevi worship, or matrimonial records.
