Surname directory

Surnames (atak) and ancestral villages named in the published community material cited by the article on gotras, surnames and genealogy. This is a reference aid, not a genealogical authority.

Read this before using the directory
  • A surname alone never establishes caste membership or gotra. Several entries below also occur among neighbouring communities, and titles such as Joshi, Mehta and Kothari are shared across many Gujarati communities.
  • Entries come from the secondary sources cited in the article [4][5][6][7] (a community registry website, a web-encyclopedia synthesis and a field report). They have not been checked against primary family records for this directory.
  • Only collective information (surnames, villages) is listed. No individuals, phone numbers or addresses are published.
