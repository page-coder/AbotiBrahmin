# Image Credits and Licensing Documentation

All photographs and visual media integrated into the AbotiBrahmin Heritage Encyclopedia are curated from verified, openly licensed repositories (principally Wikimedia Commons) in strict accordance with their respective Creative Commons and Public Domain licenses.

### Methodological Rule on Visual Media
Representative photographs of architectural landmarks (such as the Dwarkadhish Temple), rivers (Gomti Sangam), and deities (Bhagwan Parshuram) illustrate sacred geography, iconography, and physical settings. **In accordance with historical research ethics, no general photograph is presented as standalone empirical proof of a specific genealogical or sub-caste claim.**
