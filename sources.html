Sources, Bibliography, and Research Methodology

Primary & Scholarly Bibliography

  • Prakash, Om (2005). Cultural History of India. New Delhi: New Age International Publishers, p. 313. ISBN 978-81-224-1587-2. (Documenting Aboti Brahmins, 1228 CE / VS 1306 Rajasthan records, Dvaravati migration, and Dwarkadhish Janmashtami puja).
  • Enthoven, R. E. (1920). The Tribes and Castes of Bombay, Vol. I. Bombay: Government Central Press. (Cataloging Aboti as a subdivision of Shrimali Brahmans).
  • Census of India (1901). Census of Junagadh State, 1901. (Recording 970 Aboti Brahmins engaged in agriculture in Sheel and Keshod mahals).
  • CMPA Technical Report Series No. 30 (2018). Socio-economic Baseline Assessment at Gosabara Wetland Complex and Khijadiya Wildlife Sanctuary, Gujarat. GIZ / Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. (Documenting Aboti Brahmin settlements in Tukda Gosa, Mokar, and Virpur, agriculture, and courier networks).
  • Rao, S. R. (1999). The Lost City of Dvaraka. Aditya Prakashan. (Archaeological Survey of India excavations at Dwarkadhish Temple).
  • Aboti Parivar Community Directory & Dwij Samhita (2015). Published by the Aboti Brahmin Vikas Trust, documenting family branches, gotras, and ancestral villages in Porbandar.
