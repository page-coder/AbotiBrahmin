Archive / Editorial policy

Editorial standards and transparency

This is an independent public-interest archive, not an official community authority or an academic institution. Its purpose is to make the difference between knowledge, tradition and uncertainty visible.

How claims are labelled

Documented means a claim is tied to a traceable source. Traditional account identifies a religious, oral or community narration. Interpretation is an explanation offered by a source or editor. Needs verification marks a useful lead that cannot yet be confirmed.

Sources and images

Priority is given to official institutions, scholarly work, published reference sources and accessible archival material. Supplied images are used as contextual illustrations; their presence does not prove an adjacent historical claim. Rights, creator and licence information must be confirmed before any public republication.

Corrections and contributions

Contributors should provide a source, a stable citation or clear provenance for any proposed correction. Family documents, photographs and oral accounts need the informed permission of the people who own them or are identifiable in them. Private details, living people’s data and unconsented material should not be published.

Translation status

Gujarati navigation is provided as an access aid. Full Gujarati article translations should be reviewed by a qualified Gujarati editor before publication; incomplete translations are never presented as final.
