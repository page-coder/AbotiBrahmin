## Content integrity and research limits

- Community-reported family and business associations remain attributed as community information; surnames and director records are not treated as proof of community identity.
- The courier chapter distinguishes the seven businesses and does not combine their ownership or network figures.
- The 3,054 franchisee and 154 regional-hub figures remain attributed only to Shree Maruti Integrated Logistics Limited, as reported by CARE Ratings for 31 January 2025.
- Nandan's complete founder/current ownership history and Balaji's founder/community connection remain unresolved in the existing chapter.
- The two Mahabali corporate entities are not merged without documentary proof.
- Image authorship/licensing is not verified. Review `assets/images/README.md` and `MANUAL-VERIFICATION.md` before public deployment.

## Translation status

The Gujarati directory exists and has a counterpart for each English page. This is not equivalent to complete translation parity. The original archive's own build report disclosed that many legacy Gujarati pages were reconstructed from search-index extracts. This review confirmed substantial differences in main-content lengths on several legacy pages. No claim of complete translation is made. See `TRANSLATION-STATUS.md`.
